# ⚡ Energy Club OUTR

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **Energy Club OUTR**.  
We are a student-led technical club focused on **Energy Systems, IoT, Embedded Technology, and Research-driven Innovation**.

---

## 🌱 About Us
Energy Club OUTR aims to bridge the gap between **theoretical knowledge and real-world energy applications**.  
We encourage students to build, experiment, research, and innovate in domains related to **power, energy, and smart systems**.

---

## 🎯 Our Focus Areas
- 🔌 Renewable & Sustainable Energy Systems  
- 🌐 Internet of Things (IoT) for Energy Applications  
- 🧠 Data Analytics & Machine Learning in Energy  
- ⚙️ Embedded Systems (ESP32, ESP8266, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Pico)  
- 📊 Energy Monitoring, AQI & Smart Grid Concepts  
- 📄 Technical Research & Paper Development  

---

## 🛠️ Technologies We Work With
- **Hardware:** ESP32, ESP8266, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Pico  
- **Sensors:** Energy meters, Gas sensors, Environmental sensors  
- **Programming:** Arduino C/C++, Python, MATLAB  
- **Software & Tools:** Flask, GitHub, Linux, Data Analytics tools
